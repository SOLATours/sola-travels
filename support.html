Getting started

  1. Open Settings and add your home airport, family members and loyalty programs.
  2. Go to Settings → AI & API keys, add a Claude key (from console.anthropic.com) or a Grok key (from console.x.ai), and turn on Allow sending trip details. AI usage is billed by that provider.
  3. Tap + on the Trips screen to plan a trip. The sample Boston trip shows what each section does.

Common questions

Why do I need my own API key? SOLA Travels has no servers. Your requests go straight from your device to the AI provider you choose, only after you allow it, so your trip details aren't stored by us.

Where is my data? On your iPhone or iPad only. Family and loyalty numbers are in the iOS Keychain. See the privacy policy.

How do I import Amex Travel results? In a trip, open Amex, tap Open Amex Travel, sign in, search, then tap Import this page. SOLA Travels is not affiliated with American Express.

How do I delete everything? Use Settings → Delete all data before deleting the app.
